# Staff Radio

## http://radio.local

Connect to **staff Wi-Fi**, open **[http://radio.local](http://radio.local)**, and choose a station. Press **Listen**. Give it a few seconds to start.

The radio is available on the staff network only. Keep the page open while listening.

## Playback

The bar at the top shows the station and song you are hearing. Use **Stop** to stop listening and **Play** to rejoin the live broadcast.

On a computer, use the volume slider or **Mute** in the top bar. On a phone, use the phone's volume buttons.

## Stations and music

Everyone tuned to the same station hears the same broadcast. Joining partway through a song is normal.

Select a station to see what's playing, what's next, and its song list. You can browse other stations without changing what you're hearing. Press **Listen** when you want to switch.

On a computer, the station list stays on the left while you scroll through songs. On a phone, press **Stations** at the top to open the list. It closes when you choose a station; **Close stations** closes it without making a selection.

## Song requests

Choose a station and press **Request a song**. Enter the title and artist, then press **Send request**. Suggestions appear as you type; choosing one fills in both fields.

The song list is searchable, so you can check whether a song is already there. Requests are reviewed before being added to the rotation. They won't play straight away.

To suggest another station, use **Request a station** below the station list. On a phone, open **Stations** to find it.

## Connection trouble

If the page won't open, check that you're on **staff Wi-Fi** and using **http://radio.local**.

If the music cuts out, press **Reconnect**. On a phone, this button appears with a playback message below the controls. You can also press **Stop**, then **Play**. If that doesn't work, reload the page and press **Listen** on your station.
