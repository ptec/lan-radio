Playback diagnostics

Updates every five seconds while open. Counters cover each station's current runtime and reset when the service restarts or the station is recreated. Late chunks mean the broadcaster missed its schedule by over 100 ms; this can indicate CPU, disk, database, or scheduling delays. Slow-listener disconnects mean a server-side queue filled; they can indicate a blocked connection or server congestion, not necessarily Wi-Fi trouble. These measurements do not measure the browser's audio buffer.

Cache maintenance

The cache holds downloaded audio so songs can play without being downloaded again. Use these tools to reclaim space after catalog changes or rebuild the audio library.

Removes audio files that no song in the local catalog uses. Files used by approved, pending, or rejected songs are kept. Sync with Sheets first if you have removed or changed songs there.
Deletes all cached recordings and resets download failures. Approved songs on approved stations download again through the normal queue. Use this when you want to rebuild the entire audio library; listening may be interrupted until songs are ready again.

Both options show the number of files and their size before asking you to confirm deletion. Neither deletes songs, notes, or moderation decisions from the catalog or spreadsheet. Files currently in use may need another cleanup attempt; downloads already in progress are handled by their workers.

iTunes metadata review

Checks song titles and artists against iTunes to help find spelling, capitalization, or naming differences. A match requires both fields to match a search result exactly. A missing match is a reason to review the entry, not proof that it is wrong.

Checks songs without a saved metadata result and retries failed searches. Previously completed checks are skipped, including songs already flagged for review.
Checks only songs awaiting approval that have no metadata result or whose last search failed. Completed checks are skipped.
Runs a fresh check of every distinct title and artist in the catalog, replacing previous results. This is the slowest option; use it when you deliberately want to refresh everything.
Clears the station and text search filters and shows songs marked Needs metadata review. It does not start a scan. Use the table's Show filter to see failed searches or unscanned songs.

To correct an entry, click its title or artist and choose an iTunes suggestion, or edit it yourself. Then use Save & sync. To check just that song, open Details / rescan in its Checks column and choose Rescan metadata. Save any edits before scanning.

Bulk scans cover all stations regardless of the table filters. Results survive restarts, and identical title/artist pairs share a check. Both iTunes tools update from the same lookup, so you do not need to run both. Only one scan runs at a time; searches are paced to avoid overwhelming iTunes. Scanning does not edit titles, artists, or approval decisions.

iTunes explicit-content review

Looks up iTunes content ratings to help moderators decide which songs need closer listening. These ratings describe matching catalog entries, not the audio downloaded from YouTube. Scanning never approves or rejects a song.

Checks songs without a saved explicit-content result and retries failed searches. Completed results, including unknown ratings, are kept.
Checks only songs awaiting approval that have no explicit-content result or whose last search failed. Completed checks are skipped.
Refreshes the rating for every distinct title and artist in the catalog, including songs checked before. Allow extra time for a large catalog.
Clears the station and text search filters and shows songs marked explicit or with conflicting ratings. It does not start a scan. Use the table's Show filter to find unknown or unchecked ratings.

Reading the results: Explicit means a matching entry is marked explicit. Edited version means an edited recording is listed. Not marked explicit means the matching entry carries that rating; it does not verify your downloaded copy. Conflicting versions means matching results disagree. Rating unknown means there is no exact match or usable rating. Search failed means the lookup could not complete; retry it later.

Use Details / rescan in a song's Checks column to see the returned recordings or choose Rescan explicit rating. Preview the audio and use the YouTube ID to select the intended recording before making your moderation decision.

Bulk scans cover all stations regardless of the table filters. Both iTunes tools share their lookup and update together. Saved results are reused unless you request a rescan; changing the title or artist requires a check of the new details.
